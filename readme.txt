SUMO to LCC Mapping
===================

Author: Anthropic Claude 5.5
Human Advisor: Thomas Mark Schaefer

A two-way mapping between the Suggested Upper Merged Ontology (SUMO)
and the Library of Congress Classification (LCC).

Contents
--------
merged_mapping.md - Normalized two-way mapping, coverage, and mapping rules.
hole_register.md  - Gaps, granularity mismatches, defects, and false friends.
debate_log.md     - Advocate discussions, concessions, and coordinator rulings.

Erratum 2026-10-30: four of the seven SUMO defects in hole_register.md §4 were revised after re-checking against SUMO commit 8bb7a9c. See the note at the top of that file.

The mapping covers SUMO's top-level subclass tree through depth 4 and
the LCC classification outlines. Source versions and verification details
are recorded in the documents. Companion databases, CSVs, and work scripts
mentioned in the documents are not included in this repository.
